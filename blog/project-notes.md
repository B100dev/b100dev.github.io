# Project notes and design choices

A lot of design decisions are really just decisions about attention.

I try to keep interfaces simple so people can focus on the content instead of the chrome around it. A page should feel easy to read, easy to use, and easy to trust.

## The guidelines I keep returning to

- Use enough contrast to be readable
- Keep spacing consistent
- Let the content lead
- Remove the unnecessary

The most useful things are rarely the loudest ones.
