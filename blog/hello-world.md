# Hello world, again

Starting a new project is always a little strange. There is the excitement of a blank page, the pressure to make it perfect, and the quiet realization that the best way forward is to keep it simple.

I like the idea of making small, useful things. A clean portfolio. A tiny tool. A little writing habit. Nothing noisy. Nothing overbuilt.

This site is meant to be a place for that kind of work: clear, useful, and honest.

## What I want to keep doing

- Ship ideas quickly
- Keep the design quiet and readable
- Make tools that solve real problems
- Write about what I learn

Starting again is not a reset. It is just a clearer version of the same intention.
