🛠 How to Use

1. Drag & Drop
Drag any component from the left panel onto the canvas.

2. Connect Wires
Select the Wire tool, then click a pin (gold dot) and click another pin to connect them with a copper trace.

3. Move Components
With the Select tool, drag components to reposition them.

4. Delete
With the Delete tool, click a component or wire to remove it.

5. Toggle Switch
With the Select tool, click a switch to open or close it.

6. Simulate
Click ▶ Simulate — LEDs and buzzers activate when a complete powered loop is detected.
